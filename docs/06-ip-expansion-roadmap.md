# ZOMBIE ZERO · IP Expansion Plan (initial)
Version: 2026-11-13. Strategic proposal; no new products are claimed shipped.

## Positioning
**Franchise name:** ZOMBIE ZERO
**Core appeal:** comically expressive square-headed undead and human survivors, colourful industrial monsters, a recognisable hinged-head / pink-brain icon, and energetic arcade encounters.
**Art contract:** one shared canonical model bible so every product reads as the same IP, regardless of game genre.

## Franchise architecture
- **ZOMBIE ZERO**: master brand, canonical logo, unified character identities.
- **ZOMBIE ZERO: ARCADE**: current active product (ball-reflection / zombie-defense game). Retain existing 50 stages + endless mode; focus here before spinning up another codebase.
- **ZOMBIE ZERO: COLLECTION**: in-game codex/character catalogue and later collectible expression, not a committed stand-alone gacha game.
- **ZOMBIE ZERO: PANIC**: a possible high-energy action/arena follow-up informed by supplied historic gameplay footage; *working title only* until naming and rights review.
- **ZOMBIE ZERO SHORTS / GOODS**: short-format animated gags, stickers, printable/poster assets, only after distinctive character assets and commercial rights are verified.

## Character family / first-wave roster
1. **Signature zombie**: lime-green cube, rigid dark lid, white/pink eyes, exposed pink brain; flagship face and sticker/emote.
2. **Blue giant**: wide cyan head/body, huge jaw, marquee/mini-boss silhouette.
3. **Trash-can resident**: grey metal bin shell, deep purple eyes/hands, recognizable prop-monster.
4. **Red barrel**: compact red hazard/utility character.
5. **Black hazard barrel**: charcoal drum + yellow hazard sign; identity design distinct from red.
6. **Red and yellow cube containers**: exposed brains / openable lids.
7. **Cardboard and wooden crate**: box family differentiated by tape and wooden struts.
8. **Human trio**: cap/racket, blonde and red-haired/glasses/skull-shirt mini heroes.
Names beyond the original art are production handles, not canon character names until approved.

## Priorities and gates
**Gate A · Canon first**
- Define mascot silhouettes, 3/4 turnarounds, size ratios, palettes, logo treatment, expression sheets.
- Produce one canonical approved green zombie atlas and one hero.
- No speculative broadening of the roster before these are approved.

**Gate B · Flagship visual release**
- Use the original-cast art in current arcade gameplay, with an in-game codex/character gallery as the first IP extension.
- Add profile collectibles as *cosmetics/content*, not pressure-driven monetization.
- Capture player feedback on character recall, clarity and game fun.

**Gate C · Low-cost media test**
- Four short gags (brain lid malfunction; trash-can confusion; red vs black drum rivalry; human trio response) and an original sticker/emote pack.
- One strong key visual, poster and store kit. Reuse already approved models/render passes.
- Evaluate content engagement and production cost before committing to external merch.

**Gate D · Next game / licensing**
- Prototype only after flagship is visually stable and has enough gameplay evidence.
- Candidate formats: short-session crowd survival, arena boss rush, co-op slapstick action. None is committed.
- Negotiate collaborations/licensing only with documented chain of title and clear brand guidelines.

## IP governance
- Maintain an asset register: filename, creative origin, known creator, permission status, commercial-use scope, variants and checksums.
- Confirm who owns/controls pre-existing logo, concept art, 3D models, videos, music and any past employer/client material. User supply alone does not establish rights.
- Trademark / title search, localization and partner contract review precede paid release or merchandise launch; do not present existing registrations as facts.
- All variants inherit the canonical green zombie, 3/4 low-poly render style, bright colour blocks and playful expression language.

## Scope discipline
Do not replace a working 50-stage arcade with a speculative full 3D rebuild in one step. Prioritize: **asset audit → flagship art → playable visual swap → original cast → codex → media experiment → next game**.
